# Blog sample redesign notes

## Research sources

- Inkwell: The Paris Review redesign/archive note, used for quiet literary navigation, archive weight, and serif reading tone: https://www.theparisreview.org/blog/2016/11/28/new-paris-review-look-great-paris-review-taste/
- Prism: It's Nice That, used for image-led visual-culture taxonomy and strong discipline/tag browsing: https://www.itsnicethat.com/
- Mono: The Markup and Rest of World, used for restrained technical/public-interest publishing, methodology cues, and lightweight product discipline: https://themarkup.org/ and https://restofworld.org/about/
- Press: Nieman Lab opinion/news design guidance, used for explicit opinion labeling and civic newspaper rules: https://www.niemanlab.org/2017/12/better-design-helps-differentiate-opinion-and-news/
- Velvet: Monocle about page, used for independent travel/culture magazine tone and place-led editorial sensibility: https://monocle.com/about-us/
- Halo: Pitchfork and Wire, used for music criticism, archive/review conventions, and independent music/sound culture positioning: https://pitchfork.com/ and https://wire.primedirection.org/about/

## Per-sample decisions

- `inkwell-blog`: quiet white/cobalt literary notebook. Added a left marginal note/photo rail, serif titles, thin rules, and more specific Korean literary copy.
- `prism-blog`: yellow/black image-led visual culture journal. Replaced the generic visual shell with local image fields and a feature-led magazine grid.
- `mono-devlog`: graphite/green engineering field log. Reworked list into a dense log/table rhythm, flattened green accents, and rewrote lead content around believable build notes.
- `press-brut`: independent civic/opinion newspaper. Preserved filter/read/write interactions, shifted headline language to civic editorial, changed orange brutalism to black/red newspaper rules and asymmetric rows.
- `velvet-blog`: dusty pink/white travel/place journal. Replaced the emoji-first aesthetic with real local travel photos, serif editorial hierarchy, and place mood labels.
- `halo-neon`: black/lime underground music zine. Replaced the old tech skin with local music media and rewrote content around clubs, labels, playlists, and posters.
