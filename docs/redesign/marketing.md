# Marketing Sample Redesign Notes

## Shared Research: Why AI Mockups Look Generic

- Generic AI frontend mockups tend to rely on abstract spectacle instead of product evidence: repeated rounded cards, gradient hero panels, decorative blobs, fake partner logos, unsupported metrics, and vague "AI-powered" copy.
- This group moves the first screen toward concrete artifacts: a release plan table, a photographed audio device, and a resource schedule grid.
- Representative concept generated with ImageGen: `docs/redesign/marketing-concept.png`.

## Aurora Landing

Sources:
- FigJam, online collaborative whiteboard for planning and collaboration: https://www.figma.com/figjam/
- Miro collaborative whiteboard positioning around real-time/asynchronous teamwork: https://miro.com/whiteboard/
- Asana product launch workflow reference: https://asana.com/uses/product-launch

Decisions:
- Recast Aurora as a release collaboration workspace, not a vague AI SaaS platform.
- Removed fake trust-logo strip, glass panels, purple gradients, animated blobs, and unsupported productivity metrics.
- First viewport now shows a native, selectable release-plan demo with owners, teams, due dates, status, comments, and a selected blocker panel.
- Palette: true white, ink, cobalt. Recommended registry style label: `white/ink/cobalt release-planning SaaS`.
